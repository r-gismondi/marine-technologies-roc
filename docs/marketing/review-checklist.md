# External publication review checklist

Before any ROC material leaves Marine Technologies (website, press, brochure, partner deck):

- [ ] **Accuracy**: Matches actual services and contracts; no implied coverage beyond agreements.
- [ ] **Safety/regulatory**: No statements that could be read as replacing statutory obligations (watchkeeping, classification, etc.) without Legal review.
- [ ] **Claims**: No unverified performance claims (uptime, response time) unless backed by a published Service Level Agreement (SLA) or approved metrics.
- [ ] **Data/privacy**: No customer or vessel identifiers in public drafts; sample data anonymized.
- [ ] **Approvals**: Commercial and/or Legal sign-off recorded (name, date).
- [ ] **Version**: Document version and date on published Portable Document Format (PDF) files / web copy.

Keep approval records per company document control policy.
