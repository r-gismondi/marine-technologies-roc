# Marine Technologies — Remote Operations Center (ROC)

_Draft — not approved for external use until Commercial/Legal review._

## What ROC is

The **Remote Operations Center** is Marine Technologies’ coordinated shore-side hub for remote operational support—bringing together aligned processes for customer assistance, remote monitoring, and responsible handling of operational data from vessels to shore.

## What customers gain

- **Consistent response** through coordinated procedures and clear escalation paths.
- **Operational awareness** supported by structured monitoring and qualified personnel.
- **Disciplined data stewardship** aligned with contracts and regulatory expectations.

## What ROC is not

ROC does not replace Marine Technologies’ engineering authority for product safety or replace on-board crew responsibilities; it **supports** compliant remote operations within agreed boundaries.
