# ISO 9001 / QMS alignment for ROC documentation

Marine Technologies is ISO 9001 certified. ROC documentation must support the company Quality Management System (QMS); it must not create a parallel or conflicting quality system.

Use this page as an alignment checklist for ROC-owned procedures, goals, records, and training material. Final approval, numbering, retention, and controlled-copy handling must follow Marine Technologies Document Control and Quality requirements.

## Scope of this guide

This guide applies to ROC documentation in this repository, including:

- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
- Goals and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
- Training and competency material.
- Records references and evidence expectations.
- Migration and internal communication documents.

This guide is not a substitute for Marine Technologies' official Quality Manual, Document Control procedure, internal audit procedure, corrective-action process, or record-retention policy.

## Controlled document expectations

Each ROC SOP should include:

- Document identifier.
- Title.
- Version.
- Status (`Draft`, `Approved`, `Retired`, or company-equivalent status).
- Owner.
- Approver or approval authority.
- Effective date.
- Review cycle.
- Last reviewed date.
- Controlled-copy note.
- Revision history with reason for change.

Drafts in this repository are working documents until approved through Marine Technologies Document Control or the applicable Quality process.

## Records expectations

ROC documentation should not store controlled records directly unless Document Control approves that location.

Instead, ROC documents should identify:

- Which record must exist.
- Where the controlled record is stored.
- Who owns the record.
- Which retention rule applies.
- Who may access it.
- Which SOP or process creates or uses it.

Use the [records matrix](records-matrix.md) for this mapping.

## Training and competence expectations

Before an SOP is marked approved, confirm:

- Affected roles are identified.
- Training or read-and-acknowledge requirements are defined.
- Competence evidence is stored in the approved Human Resources (HR), learning, or quality system.
- Retraining triggers are defined for major revisions, incidents, audit findings, or tool changes.

## Change control expectations

Changes to approved SOPs should:

- Follow Marine Technologies change-control and Document Control requirements.
- Include a reason for change in revision history.
- Identify whether retraining or acknowledgment is required.
- Reference the relevant change request, audit finding, incident, or corrective action in the official system of record.

## Audit readiness

For ISO 9001 audits, ROC should be able to show:

- Approved current SOPs and revision history.
- Evidence of review and approval.
- Training / acknowledgment evidence for affected roles.
- Records created by each SOP.
- Metrics and review cadence for quality objectives.
- Evidence that nonconformities, procedure gaps, and corrective actions are handled in the official company system.
