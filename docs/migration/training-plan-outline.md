# Training plan outline (draft)

Tailor per person based on **stream** assignment under the ROC Supervisor: Customer support | Information Technology (IT) | Monitoring | Remote operations support | Data/shore pipeline.

## Universal onboarding (all Agents)

- ROC charter and principles ([charter.md](../strategy/charter.md))
- Class-assisted operating concept ([class-and-conops.md](../strategy/class-and-conops.md)) — one controller, onboard override, link-loss reversion
- Security and data-handling rules (link internal security training)
- Ticketing and escalation basics ([SOP-ROC-001](../procedures/SOP-ROC-001-incident-management-and-escalation.md))
- Office-hours handoff and on-call continuity expectations ([SOP-ROC-002](../procedures/SOP-ROC-002-office-hours-handoff-and-on-call-continuity.md))
- Major incident communications basics ([SOP-ROC-006](../procedures/SOP-ROC-006-major-incident-communications-internal.md))

## Stream-specific modules

| Module | Outcomes |
|--------|----------|
| Customer support | Case ownership, tone, escalation criteria ([SOP-ROC-004](../procedures/SOP-ROC-004-customer-inquiry-routing-and-ownership.md)) |
| Information Technology (IT) stream | Access workflows, tooling administration, platform support discipline, no unmanaged tooling outside ROC governance |
| Monitoring | Alarm philosophy, runbooks, fatigue controls |
| Remote operations support | Authorization boundaries, engineering liaison ([SOP-ROC-003](../procedures/SOP-ROC-003-remote-intervention-assisted-operations-authorization.md)) |
| Data / shore pipeline | Retention, export approval, audit trail ([SOP-ROC-005](../procedures/SOP-ROC-005-data-export-retention-access-requests.md)) |

## Competency sign-off

- **Supervisor** (or delegate) confirms checklist completion per stream.
- Re-training triggers: major product release, procedure revision, or incident learning review.
